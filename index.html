01

how does this work?

three steps, one shark.

step
01

build something blahaj

a web app, game, tool, visualizer, creative project, whatever you feel like. it just needs a blahaj theme and actual effort behind it. no one's going to count your open tabs.

step
02

clock around 7–8 hours

that's the bar. you can spread the time across a couple of projects, they all count together. each set of qualifying hours = one blahaj. so if you really go for it, you can stack them.

step
03

submit and we ship it

send us your project link and a hours log. we review it, and if it checks out, a blahaj gets shipped straight to your door. can't ship to your region? we'll give you a $30 grant to buy one yourself.

heads up about customs: depending on where you live your package might get hit with import fees on arrival. we will try to help but we can't always cover them. if customs is going to be a real problem for you, message us in #haj4ever-help before you submit.
