01. what is this

you ship a blåhaj project.
we ship you a blåhaj.

Haj4ever is a Hack Club YSWS (You Ship, We Ship). Build anything related to Blåhaj, track your hours with Hackatime, and Hack Club will mail you a real IKEA Blåhaj shark. Free international shipping. No catch.

02. how it works

three steps to your shark.

1. build something
Make any Blåhaj-related project, software, hardware, art, game, whatever you want. It just has to be made by you, not vibe-coded.
2. track your hours
Log your time using Hackatime. You need around 7-8 hours of tracked work. If you're in a team, everyone needs their own commits and hours.
3. get your blåhaj
Submit your project, we review it, and if it's good Hack Club ships you a real IKEA Blåhaj straight to your door.
03. rules

the fine print.

Open to teenagers aged 13-18. You'll need to verify your age (student or government ID) before we ship.
Your project has to be Blåhaj-related. Software, hardware, art, anything goes as long as it's themed.
No vibe coding. AI can help (max 30% usage) but the project needs to be mostly yours. Declare any AI use in your submission.
No double-dipping: you can't submit the same project to another Hack Club YSWS.
Teams of up to 5 are cool. Each member needs separate commits and Hackatime hours.
Hack Club covers international shipping. Customs fees depend on your country.
